01 Overview

PlatePal is a chat assistant for everyday Indian meals. A person sends a photo of the plate and, if they want a shop, a location pin. The bot names the dishes, waits for a correction, then compares that meal with their daily need.

The reply is a short gap, up to three low-cost foods, and a map link. It is an estimate from a photo and a recipe table, not a diagnosis.

02 The gap

A full plate can still be short on iron, protein, calcium, folate, or B12. Calorie apps are built around Western dishes and stop at the number. They do not use Indian home recipes, and they do not say which shop is close.

PlatePal starts at that next step: name the shortfall, then name a food you can buy nearby.

04 Two programs

Bot A thin relay. It downloads the photo, calls the engine, and formats the reply. No nutrition logic lives here.
Engine A separate API. It holds the vision key, matches dishes, sums nutrients, and ranks foods by the gap.
The vision model is asked only for dish names and a rough portion. Iron, protein, and the rest come from the food table, so a number can be traced to a row.

Telegram is the build channel: it is free and accepts photos, pins, and buttons. The same engine can sit behind WhatsApp later. Stack for the prototype: Python, FastAPI, SQLite.

05 The numbers

  • DishesIndian Nutrient Databank, cooked recipes
  • Raw foodsIFCT 2017, ICMR–NIN
  • TargetsICMR–NIN RDA 2020, by age, sex, and weight
  • B12A second food table. Marked lower confidence.
  • ShopsThe user’s pin, to a market or kirana

Tamil home dishes missing from the databank — pongal, poriyal, kootu, kuzhambu, keerai — are added from their ingredients. A photo cannot see the exact portion, so nothing is counted until the person confirms.

07 What it does

  • Confirm firstThe meal counts only after you accept the list.
  • Weekly scoreIron, protein, calcium, folate, and B12.
  • Under ₹30Three foods by missing nutrient per rupee.
  • Nearest shopA pin returns a market or kirana.
  • Iron pairingSuggests vitamin C with iron-rich food.
  • Tamil and voiceTyped meals, or a voice note.
  • Family potOne meal, split across several people.
  • Report on requestA summary only when the user asks.
